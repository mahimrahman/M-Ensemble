**Description**:

M’Ensemble brings your mosque into your pocket.

Follow the mosques you attend and get one feed of everything they publish:
events, classes, announcements and calls for volunteers. See today's adhan and
iqamah times as the mosque actually prays them, with the full month a tap away.

Volunteering is simple: claim a shift in one tap, check in at the door with a
QR code, and keep a record of the hours you've given. If plans change, withdraw
early so someone else can step in.

For mosque coordinators, M’Ensemble is a lightweight management tool: publish
events and classes with a poster, see who has signed up, check volunteers in,
send announcements to everyone who follows the mosque, and keep the prayer
table up to date.

Mosques that choose to can also receive donations through the app. Payments are
handled by Stripe in your browser; receipts come from the mosque.

• Prayer and iqamah times for the mosques you follow
• Events, classes and announcements in one feed
• Volunteer sign-ups with QR check-in and service hours
• Coordinator tools: publish, manage sign-ups, broadcast
• French, English and Arabic
• Free for members

Built in Quebec, for communities across Canada.
