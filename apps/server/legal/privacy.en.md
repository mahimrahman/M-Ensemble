# Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 — effective November 1, 2026

M'Ensemble ("we", "us") is a Quebec, Canada company that publishes the M'Ensemble mobile application and its companion services (the "Service"). The Service helps mosques publish programs, events and volunteer shifts, and helps people find and take part in them. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, how long we keep it, and the rights you have over it under Quebec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (Law 25) and Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

If anything here is unclear, write to our Privacy Officer at the address in the "Contact" section. We answer within 30 days.

## Who is responsible

M'Ensemble is responsible for the personal information collected through the Service. Our Privacy Officer is the person in charge of protecting it and of answering your requests.

Each mosque that uses the Service is separately responsible for how its coordinators use the information they can see about members of their own community (for example, who signed up for a shift). We provide the tools; the mosque decides how to use them within these terms.

## What we collect

We collect only what the Service needs to work. In each case the information comes from you, or is generated by your use of the app.

**Account information.** Your name, email address and a password (stored only as a one-way hash — we never see or store the password itself). Optionally, the interests you pick during onboarding so the feed can be filtered. Whether you have confirmed your email address.

**Activity.** The mosques you follow, the posts you like, the shifts and events you sign up for, when you checked in, and when you withdrew. Coordinators see this for their own mosque so they can plan and run the activity you joined.

**Notifications.** A device push token, if you allow notifications, and your notification preferences. The notifications we have sent you are kept in your in-app inbox.

**Location.** With your permission, the app reads your device's approximate location once to pick the city whose mosques to show. We do not store your coordinates on our servers; only the city you end up browsing is sent with your requests.

**Camera.** With your permission, the app uses the camera to scan a QR code at check-in. Images are processed on the device and never uploaded.

**Photos and uploads.** Coordinators may upload a poster image for a post. Uploaded files are stored with the post and shown to anyone who can see it.

**Donations.** When you donate to a mosque, payment is processed by Stripe, Inc. We receive the amount, currency, the mosque it went to, and a reference number. We do not receive or store your card number. Stripe's own privacy policy governs the information you give Stripe.

**Sponsored cards.** Some screens show a card from a partner. We record that a card was shown and whether it was tapped, aggregated by campaign, so the partner can be billed. We do not share who saw or tapped a card with the partner.

**Technical information.** Our servers keep standard request logs (IP address, time, endpoint, response code) for security and troubleshooting, and error reports (which may include your account identifier) so we can fix bugs.

## Why we collect it

We use personal information to:

- create and secure your account, and let you sign in;
- show you programs and events from the mosques you follow, in the city you chose;
- let you sign up for, check in to and withdraw from activities, and let coordinators run them;
- send the notifications you have opted into;
- process donations through Stripe and give the mosque a record of them;
- bill mosques for their subscription and partners for their campaigns;
- keep the Service secure, detect abuse, and fix problems;
- answer your questions and requests;
- meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use it for advertising profiles. We do not combine it with information bought from third parties.

## Consent

By creating an account you consent to the collection and use described here. Location, camera and notifications each ask for your permission separately, through your device, and each can be refused or withdrawn in your device settings without losing access to the rest of the Service.

You may withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account (see "Your rights"). Some information may have to be kept after that where the law requires it — for example donation records a mosque must retain for tax purposes.

## Who we share it with

**Mosques you interact with.** A mosque's coordinators can see your name, your signups and check-ins at that mosque, and your reliability record there. They cannot see your email address, password, interests, or activity at other mosques.

**Service providers.** We use a small number of providers to run the Service, each bound by contract to use the information only on our instructions: a cloud hosting provider and database host in Canada; a file storage provider for uploaded posters; Stripe for payments; an email delivery provider for account emails; Expo for push notification delivery; and an error-reporting service. A current list is available from our Privacy Officer.

**Legal.** We disclose information when the law requires it, to respond to a valid court order, or to protect the safety of a person.

**Business transfer.** If M'Ensemble is acquired or merges, your information may be transferred to the new owner under this same policy. We would tell you before that happened.

## Where it is stored

Your information is stored on servers located in Canada. Some providers listed above (Stripe, Expo, email delivery, error reporting) process data in the United States. Where information leaves Quebec we have assessed, as Law 25 requires, that it receives protection equivalent to what the law provides here, and we rely on contractual commitments from those providers.

## How long we keep it

- **Account information**: for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account we remove your name, email, password, interests and push token within 30 days.
- **Activity history** (signups, check-ins): kept in anonymised form after deletion, so a mosque's attendance records stay accurate. The anonymised rows no longer identify you.
- **Donation records**: kept for 7 years as tax law requires, with the mosque as the party of record.
- **Request logs**: 90 days.
- **Error reports**: 90 days.
- **Emails we send you** (reset and confirmation links): the tokens expire within 24 hours and are deleted shortly after.
- **Backups**: encrypted database backups are kept for 35 days and then destroyed.

## Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit. Access to production systems is limited to the people who operate the Service and is logged. Password reset and email confirmation links are single-use and expire. If we ever learn of a breach that presents a risk of serious injury, we will notify the Commission d'accès à l'information and the affected people as Law 25 requires.

## Your rights

Under Law 25 and PIPEDA you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal information we hold about you. The app's "Export my data" button gives you a complete copy as a file; you may also ask our Privacy Officer.
- **Correct** information that is inaccurate or incomplete. Your name and interests can be edited in the app; for anything else, contact us.
- **Delete** your account and personal information. "Delete my account" in the app does this immediately, subject to the retention rules above. If you are the only coordinator of a mosque you will be asked to hand over coordination first, so the mosque is not left without anyone to run it.
- **Withdraw consent** for location, camera and notifications through your device settings, or for everything by deleting your account.
- **Portability**: the export is in a structured, commonly used format (JSON).
- **Complain**: if you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing.

## Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. A person under 14 may not create an account without the consent of a parent or guardian, as Quebec law requires. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we will update the version and effective date at the top and tell you in the app before the change takes effect. Continuing to use the Service after that date means you accept the new version.
